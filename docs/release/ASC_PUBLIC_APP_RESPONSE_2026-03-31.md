## Guideline 3.2 — Business

DVD Valet is intended for public App Store distribution.

1. The app is not restricted to a single company or organization.
2. The app is not limited to a closed list of organizations. Any individual user can download it from the App Store and begin using it immediately.
3. The app's core public features are scanning DVD and Blu-ray UPC barcodes, saving titles to a personal library, and reviewing previously saved scans on device.
4. Users obtain access by downloading the app from the App Store. A saved local profile is optional and is not required before scanning DVDs or using the library.
5. The current app experience does not require organization onboarding, invitation, account approval, or enterprise provisioning.
6. The app does not include organization-restricted paid content.
